Privacy Policy

Closet · Effective October 11, 2026

Closet is an app that keeps a record of the clothes you own and includes an AI stylist that suggests outfits from them. It is made by Peter Chow, an independent developer. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who helps process it, and the choices you have. The short version: your closet is yours, it is used only to run the app for you, and it is never sold or used for advertising.

What we collect

  • Account details. When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive an account identifier and the email address the provider shares with us (with Apple, this can be a private relay address). Apple may also share your name the first time you sign in.
  • Your closet. The items you add and their details, such as brand, name, color, size, fabric, style number and notes, plus the photos you take of clothing and care labels.
  • Stylist conversations. The messages you send the stylist, any photos you attach, the stylist's replies, and short "style notes" the stylist keeps about your preferences so its advice improves.
  • Usage counts. How many stylist messages you send each day and the cost of processing them, so we can enforce the daily limit and keep the service affordable.

We do not collect your location, contacts, browsing activity, or advertising identifiers, and the app contains no third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

How we use it

  • To show your closet on your devices and keep it in sync.
  • To identify clothing from photos of care labels and find matching product details.
  • To let the stylist answer your questions using what you own.
  • To keep the service working, secure, and within its daily limits.

We do not sell your information, share it for advertising, or use it to build profiles for anyone else.

Who processes it

  • Supabase hosts the app's database, sign-in, and photo storage (servers in the United States).
  • Anthropic provides the AI (Claude) behind the stylist and label reading. When you message the stylist or add an item, the relevant text and photos are sent to Anthropic to generate a response. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, this data is not used to train their models.
  • Apple and Google handle sign-in when you choose them, under their own privacy policies.

Product details and images for clothing are looked up from public brand websites. Nothing about you is sent to those sites.

How long we keep it

  • Your closet, stylist conversations and style notes are kept until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Photos you send to the stylist are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • You can clear your stylist chat and edit or delete your style notes at any time in Settings.

Your choices

  • Delete your account anytime in Settings. This permanently deletes your account, your closet, your photos, your stylist conversations and your style notes from our servers.
  • Remove individual items from your closet at any time.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data or to correct or delete it by contacting us at the address below.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Each account can only access its own data: the database enforces this on every request, and photos are stored privately. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you if a breach affects it.

Children

Closet is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we will update the date above and let you know in the app.
