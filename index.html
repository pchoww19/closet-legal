Kloova

Your closet, on your phone.

Privacy Policy · Support

Kloova is a 3D walk-in closet on your iPhone that holds exactly the clothes you own. Add a piece by snapping its care label, and AI reads the tag and finds the exact product. Or just paste a product link.

Then ask the stylist, which knows your closet, what to wear, what to pack, or whether something is worth buying. Kloova is free, and you sign in with Apple or Google.
