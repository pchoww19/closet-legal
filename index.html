Privacy Policy

Closet · Updated October 11, 2026

What we collect

Your sign-in email from Apple or Google, the clothing you add (details and photos), and your conversations with the stylist, including any photos you send it.

How we use it

Only to run the app for you: to show and sync your closet, identify clothing from care-label photos, and answer your stylist questions. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking.

Who processes it

  • Supabase stores your data and photos.
  • Anthropic (Claude) powers the stylist and label reading, and receives the text and photos needed for each request. It does not use them to train its models.
  • Apple or Google handle sign-in.

Keeping and deleting it

Your data stays until you delete it. Photos sent to the stylist are deleted after 30 days. Delete your account anytime in Settings to permanently erase everything.
